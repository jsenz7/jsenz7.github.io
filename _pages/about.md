I’m **Sen Zhang (张森)** — a researcher and engineer working at the intersection of **smart textiles**, **soft robotics**, and **wearable sensing systems**. I am currently a **Postdoctoral Scholar** in Electrical &amp; Computer Engineering at the **University of Washington**, working with **Prof. Yiyue Luo**.

My research focuses on creating **machine-knitted multimodal sensing textiles** that can measure muscle activity (EMG), joint motion, pressure, and physiological signals, while also enabling **haptic feedback** and closed-loop interaction. I design wearable systems that are soft, comfortable, and manufacturable at scale, with the goal of supporting long-term **in-home rehabilitation and health monitoring**.

I am particularly interested in bridging advanced textile manufacturing with **human–computer interaction, neurorehabilitation, assistive technologies, and soft robotics**, turning fabrics into intelligent interfaces between the body and digital systems.

---

## 🔬 Research Directions

- **Multimodal smart textiles (EMG, EIT, motion sensing)**  
  Knitted systems that integrate electrical impedance tomography, muscle sensing, and kinematic tracking for real-time movement and physiological monitoring.

- **Magnetoactive and haptic textiles**  
  Machine-knitted electromagnets and magnetoactive fibers that provide bidirectional interaction — sensing touch and deformation while delivering programmable haptic feedback.

- **Wearable systems for rehabilitation**  
  Soft, user-friendly sleeves and garments for at-home physical therapy, remote assessment, and long-term motor recovery.

- **Soft robotic and electromechanical textile actuation**  
  Textile-based coils and soft magnetic actuators that enable motion, shape change, and interaction in compliant systems.

---

## 🚀 Current Projects

- **MultiSensKnit**  
  A multimodal smart textile sleeve that integrates EMG, electrical impedance tomography (EIT), joint motion sensing, and pressure feedback for **in-home upper-limb rehabilitation** and continuous monitoring.

- **MagTex**  
  A machine-knitted magnetoactive textile enabling **bidirectional human–machine interaction**, combining deformation sensing with spatially programmable haptic feedback.

- **FingerGlove**  
  A lightweight magnetic-sensing glove for **real-time gesture control**, gaming interaction, and soft haptic feedback.

## 🧩 Entrepreneurship

I am part of the **UW ECE Entrepreneurial Fellows Program**, where I work on translating textile-based sensing systems into practical health-tech products. My commercialization experience includes:

- **NSF I-Corps (regional and national)**  
- **Customer discovery interviews with 25+ clinicians, therapists, and industry professionals**  
- **A provisional patent on multimodal textile sensing systems**

My long-term goal is to bring clinically meaningful, comfortable, and scalable textile sensing systems into everyday rehabilitation and wellness workflows.
